# Credits and Sources

Capoworks ECU documentation adapts and extends material from earlier open-source ECU projects.
This page records the principal sources and explains how inherited names, images, and technical
artifacts are treated.

## FOME Wiki and Firmware

The initial documentation source is the [FOME Wiki repository](https://github.com/FOME-Tech/wiki)
and its published site, [wiki.fome.tech](https://wiki.fome.tech). The wiki was created and
maintained by the FOME community. Copyright and authorship notices remain with the original files,
and contributor history is preserved in the [upstream commit
history](https://github.com/FOME-Tech/wiki/commits/master) and this repository's
[history](https://github.com/capoworks/capoworks-ecu-docs/commits/master).

The FOME firmware source is at [FOME-Tech/fome-fw](https://github.com/FOME-Tech/fome-fw).
Capoworks documentation links to files in the
[Capoworks firmware repository](https://github.com/capoworks/capoworks-ecu) when a corresponding
path exists. Technical identifiers, source filenames, and artifact names retain their original
spelling.

## rusEFI

FOME was derived from [rusEFI](https://github.com/rusefi/rusefi), and inherited documentation
reflects rusEFI concepts, designs, and software. Thanks and credit remain with the rusEFI
contributors and maintainers. Consult the [rusEFI documentation](https://wiki.rusefi.com/) and the
upstream repository's [license notice](https://github.com/rusefi/rusefi/blob/master/LICENSE) for
the applicable source and licensing information.

preserved contributor and repository attribution.

## Archived Material and Technical Names

Archived diagrams, screenshots, images, and other inherited assets retain their original markings,
labels, and filenames. An FOME marking in an archived asset is historical provenance or a storage
reference; it does not indicate that the asset was created by Capoworks.

Capoworks distributions may retain inherited technical names for compatibility or because they
remain accurate identifiers. Examples include `fome.bin`, `fome.dfu`, `fome_console.exe`,
`fome_simulator.exe`, and `FOME_CAN_verbose.dbc`. These literals should remain unchanged when
following commands or locating files.

## Third-Party Hardware and Content

Hardware product names, board designs, manufacturer links, and third-party resources remain
credited to their original designers and producers. A board or accessory described here is not
automatically a Capoworks-produced product. Follow the linked design source, producer
documentation, and compatibility notes for that exact hardware.
