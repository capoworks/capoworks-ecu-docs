# Throttle Position Sensor

Throttle Position Sensors are used to tell Capoworks ECU the angle of the throttle blade. Most sensors are 3 wire:

1. Power (provided by the ECU power output)
2. Ground (provided by the ECU ground output)
3. Signal (sent to an ECU low-side input)

Wiring power and ground from the ECU will ensure a clean and consistent signal.

For more info on how TPS sensors operate on Electronic Throttele Bodies: [ETB Throttle Position](../ETB-TPS)
